# Singapore Financial Scam — PROTOTYPE POLICY SUPPLEMENT
Version: prototype-1.0

Educational design assumptions only. NOT rednote policy, NOT Singapore law,
NOT MAS guidance. No claim of compliance or regulator endorsement is made.
Global prototype P1–P6 always remain in force.

## SG1 | Local authority and licensing claims
Claims of MAS approval, Singapore government backing, or licensed-adviser status
in financial solicitation require independent human verification if material.
The model cannot verify registers, identities or product authenticity. A claim
alone is not a violation and local references alone are not evidence of fraud.

## SG2 | Local product context
CPF, SSB, T-bills, fixed deposits and SGD references may occur in legitimate
financial discussion. Do not equate locality, yield discussion or a bank promotion
with fraud. Claims linking these products to guaranteed speculative returns,
private transfers or authority impersonation require contextual review under P1/P2.
