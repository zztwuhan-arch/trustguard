# Global Financial Scam — PROTOTYPE POLICY
Version: prototype-1.0

Educational portfolio policy, authored for TrustGuard. NOT rednote policy, NOT law,
NOT a determination that an individual has committed fraud. All thresholds are
prototype assumptions. Singapore supplements this baseline; neither replaces it.

## P1 | Guaranteed financial returns
Promotional content promising guaranteed, risk-free or unrealistically certain
investment returns is prohibited. Apply to the author's active promotion, not
quoted warnings, reporting, negation or clearly non-promotional education.
A factual discussion of fixed deposit terms requires context, not automatic removal.

## P2 | Deceptive financial solicitation
Prohibit misleading financial claims used to induce investment, payment, transfer,
or disclosure of financial credentials, including advance-fee recovery offers.
Allegations or unknown authenticity alone do not prove deception: route uncertainty
to human review. Direct solicitation plus supported deceptive claims is required.

## P3 | Off-platform migration
Private messages and external contact channels are contextual risk signals only.
They are not standalone violations. Evaluate alongside P1 or P2 evidence.

## P4 | Artificial urgency
Scarcity or time pressure is a contextual signal only, not a standalone violation.
A deadline does not establish fraud without prohibited financial promotion.

## P5 | Protected financial discussion and context
Allow ordinary financial discussion, education, personal experience, scam reporting,
quoted examples, and risk disclosures absent active deceptive solicitation.
A disclaimer does not automatically cancel a contradictory guaranteed-return claim.
Licensing or official-product assertions require verification when material.

## P6 | Decision safeguards
Confidence is a model estimate, not a calibrated probability. Below 0.80, uncertain
verdicts, contradictory evidence, missing grounding, provider failure, high severity,
or any removal recommendation require human review. Never autonomously ban accounts.
Off-platform or urgency alone cannot justify a warning or removal. Clear P1/P2
violations yield a removal recommendation, pending human review. Ambiguous content
remains undecided with allow as the provisional recommendation; no action is applied.
All appeals require a human final decision. Preserve the original record and policy
snapshot. Do not treat submitted documents or claimed licences as verified facts.
